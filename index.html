01 — About

Infrastructure people rely on, secured end to end.

I started out on a support desk in Harare, resolving 45 tickets a day and learning that most outages are really communication problems wearing a technical costume. That grounding followed me into network engineering at Liquid Intelligent Technologies, one of Africa’s largest connectivity providers, where I spent five years on the infrastructure that carries a continent’s traffic.

My work spanned LAN/WAN troubleshooting, ONU/OLT provisioning on Huawei U2000 and NCE, fixed wireless access over LTE and microwave, and 24/7 monitoring through SolarWinds and Splunk. Along the way I kept discovering the same thing: the interesting problems were security problems. So I moved to New York to study them properly.

I now hold a Master of Science in Cybersecurity from Yeshiva University’s Katz School, backed by Azure Solutions Architect Expert, Cisco CCNP Security and Enterprise, and CompTIA Security+ credentials. I’m looking for roles in security operations, network security, or cloud infrastructure where deep network fluency is an asset rather than an afterthought.

  • Security Operations

    SIEM monitoring, alert triage, and escalation with Splunk and SolarWinds.

  • Network Engineering

    Routing, switching, firewalls, and fiber transport at carrier scale.

  • Cloud Architecture

    Azure solution design and administration at expert certification level.

Zero Trust Architecture for a University

Designed and authored a full Zero Trust Architecture for a large university — replacing implicit network trust with identity as the control plane, and writing the reference design that ties campus, data centre, and cloud into one enforcement model.
